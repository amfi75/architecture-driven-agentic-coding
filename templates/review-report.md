## Verdict

Record baseline, approved deltas, requirement preservation, unauthorized changes, each criterion and overall PASS/FAIL/BLOCKED. Missing required evidence is BLOCKED; demonstrated required failure is FAIL. PASS requires all required criteria. Preferences and unrelated future work are not new requirements.

Project learning may be recorded. Promotion into global rules, ADAC or another repository requires its own authority.
